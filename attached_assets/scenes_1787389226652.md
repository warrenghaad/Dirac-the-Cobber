# Ultra-Realistic Historical Scenes

Use this recipe for scenes of daily life, ritual, market, court, workshop, agriculture, warfare, or any moment of human activity inside a specific civilization. This is the recipe to reach for when the designer says "show me what it actually looked like when…"

## Prompt scaffold

1. **Subject line** — one sentence stating the moment: who, doing what, where, when.
2. **Civilization, period, location** — precise as always.
3. **Cast** — who is in the scene, how many, ages, roles, clothing in period-correct detail. Name garments by their actual names where possible ("kaunakes," "himation," "shendyt," "ihram," "kosode").
4. **Setting** — built environment and natural environment, with material specificity (mudbrick walls, packed-earth floor, reed-thatch roof, basalt paving, palm-frond shade).
5. **Action and staging** — what each visible figure is doing, where attention flows, what objects are in active use. Avoid frozen poses; favor mid-action.
6. **Material objects in the scene** — be specific about pots, tools, jewelry, instruments, weapons, food, textiles. This is where the visual earns its educational value.
7. **Time of day and lighting** — "early morning, low warm light through the courtyard doorway, dust motes in the beam," "late afternoon shaded loggia, even bounce light," "torchlit night ritual, deep shadows, warm flame keys."
8. **Camera and framing** — "eye-level medium-wide shot, ~35mm equivalent, observer standing across the courtyard"; "overhead three-quarter view"; "intimate close shot over the priest's shoulder."
9. **Style register** — "Ultra-realistic photographic, as if a documentary photographer were present, in the manner of careful archaeological reconstruction (Balage Balogh, Jean-Claude Golvin, or the dioramas of the Oriental Institute)."
10. **Negative cues** — "no anachronisms, no modern fabrics, no modern hairstyles, no synthetic dyes, no plastic, no glass windows unless period-appropriate, no power lines, no contemporary jewelry, no Hollywood costume, no fantasy embellishment, no swords-and-sandals epic styling, no lens flare."

## Aspect ratio

- Wide scene with environmental context → `16:9` (default)
- Vertical interior with tall architecture → `3:4`
- Square ritual focus with central figure → `1:1`

## Worked example prompt

```
A Sumerian temple morning ritual at the E-anna of Inanna in Uruk, Late Uruk period c. 3200 BCE.

Cast: a chief priestess in a long undyed wool robe with a fringed shawl drawn over her head, two junior priestesses in plain ankle-length tunics with woven belts, a temple servant boy holding a covered alabaster jar of barley beer, and a kneeling worshipper in a kaunakes skirt presenting a small alabaster votive bowl. Five figures total; period-correct dark hair, no jewelry beyond simple lapis bead strings.

Setting: the inner sanctuary court of the white temple — mudbrick walls coated in lime plaster painted with red and black geometric bands, packed earth floor, a low stepped offering altar of baked brick at the rear, a niche holding a small reed-bundle standard of Inanna (the looped reed symbol).

Action: the chief priestess is in mid-gesture pouring a thin stream of beer from a spouted alabaster vessel onto the altar; the junior priestesses stand slightly behind, eyes lowered; the servant boy steps forward with the refill jar; the worshipper kneels at the threshold, both hands extending the votive bowl.

Material objects: spouted alabaster libation vessel, plain undecorated drinking cups stacked beside the altar, the reed-bundle standard, a low woven reed mat at the threshold, small terracotta lamps with wick floats burning sesame oil.

Time and lighting: early morning, low warm sunlight angling in from the east through the high doorway, long soft shadows running west, dust motes visible in the beam, cool ambient fill from the open courtyard above.

Camera: eye-level medium-wide shot from across the sanctuary, ~35mm equivalent, observer standing quietly in the doorway.

Style: ultra-realistic photographic, as if a documentary photographer were present, in the manner of careful archaeological reconstruction (Balage Balogh / Jean-Claude Golvin).

Negative: no anachronisms, no modern fabrics, no modern hairstyles, no synthetic dyes, no plastic, no glass windows, no power lines, no contemporary jewelry, no Hollywood costume, no fantasy embellishment, no lens flare.
```

Aspect ratio: `16:9`. Model: `gpt_image_2`. Filename: `uruk_inanna_morning_libation_v1`.

## Common follow-ups to offer

- The same moment from a different camera angle (e.g. over the priestess's shoulder).
- A close-up of the libation vessel and altar surface as a separate artifact study.
- The same ritual at night by torchlight.
- A "before" or "after" companion scene (preparation in the kitchen courtyard, dispersal of worshippers).
