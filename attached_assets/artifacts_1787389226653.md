# Artifacts: Figurines, Sculpture, Statues, Reliefs, Jewelry, Textiles, Seals

Use this recipe for any portable material-culture object — votive figurines, statues, stelae, masks, vessels, cylinder seals, jewelry, inlay panels, textiles, metalwork.

## Prompt scaffold

1. **Subject line** — "[Object type] from [civilization], [period], [find context if any]."
2. **Period, region, find context** — "Early Dynastic III Sumerian votive statue from the Square Temple at Tell Asmar, c. 2700 BCE"; "Achaemenid gold rhyton with ibex protome, c. 500 BCE"; "Moche stirrup-spout vessel depicting a warrior, c. 400 CE, north coast of Peru."
3. **Material(s)** — be exact: "carved gypsum alabaster with inlaid lapis-lazuli pupils and bitumen for the irises and hair"; "hammered and chased electrum, soldered granulation"; "polished black diorite"; "fired terracotta with traces of red and white slip"; "indigo-dyed wool with undyed warp."
4. **Form description** — pose, proportions, surface details: "standing male votary, hands clasped at the chest, large inlaid eyes, long beard rendered as parallel wavy ridges, lower body cylindrical in a fringed wool kaunakes skirt with overlapping tufts." Specificity here is what separates a real artifact from a generic fantasy object.
5. **Condition** — pick one and be consistent:
   - "Pristine reconstruction as originally made"
   - "Excavated condition with weathering, mineral encrustation, missing fragments"
   - "Museum-restored condition, cleaned, mounted on a neutral plinth"
6. **Scale cue** — approximate height/length in cm or inches when known ("approx. 30 cm tall"), or a comparative cue ("fits in the palm of a hand").
7. **Framing** — "Three-quarter studio view on neutral seamless background" is the default. For reliefs, "orthographic flat-on view, raking sidelight from the upper left." For jewelry, "macro close-up, 1:1 scale, shallow depth of field."
8. **Lighting** — "soft museum studio light, neutral 5000K, gentle key from upper left, subtle fill, faint contact shadow on the plinth."
9. **Style register** — "Studio product photograph of a museum artifact, materially accurate, ultra-sharp." Alternative when reconstructing a lost original: "Archaeological reconstruction in the visual conventions of a museum reference plate."
10. **Negative cues** — "no modern museum labels, no display case glass reflections, no human hands, no rulers, no QR codes, no fantasy embellishment, no gemstone bling beyond what the period actually used, no plastic, no contemporary jewelry-display aesthetics."

## Aspect ratio

- Standing figurine / statue / tall vessel → `3:4`
- Relief panel / stele / textile → `4:3` or `16:9` depending on proportion
- Jewelry, seal, small metalwork → `1:1` (or `4:3` for elongated pieces)
- Cylinder seal **with** its modern rolled-out impression → `16:9` (seal on left, impression on right)

Material: carved gypsum alabaster, inlaid lapis-lazuli pupils set in shell sclera, bitumen pigment for the irises and the beard.

## Common follow-ups to offer

- A back-and-side rotational sheet (front / three-quarter / side / back) for full study.
- An excavated-condition variant of the same object.
- A material variant ("what if this had survived as bronze instead of stone?").
- A scale-comparison plate placing the artifact next to a period figure.
- For seals: a paired image of the seal cylinder and its rolled-out impression.
