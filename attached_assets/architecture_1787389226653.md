# Architecture (Civilization-Specific)

Use this recipe for buildings, building interiors, plans, elevations, urban views, and large architectural artifacts (ziggurats, temples, courtyards, gates, palaces, baths, observatories).

## Prompt scaffold

1. **Subject line** — "[Period/region] [building type] at [location], [viewing condition]."
2. **Period, place, date** — be precise: "Third Dynasty of Ur, c. 2100 BCE, ziggurat of Nanna at Ur"; "Achaemenid Persepolis, c. 500 BCE"; "Late Bronze Age Knossos, c. 1500 BCE"; "Nasrid Alhambra, mid-14th c."
3. **Structural specificity** — name the parts: "three receding mudbrick terraces, central monumental staircase, twin flanking stairs, summit shrine of glazed brick"; "hypostyle hall with bull-protome capitals on 20m fluted columns"; "trabeated megaron with central hearth, four columns supporting an open lantern roof."
4. **Material and construction** — "sun-dried mudbrick core with baked-brick facing, bitumen mortar, reed mats between courses"; "polished red sandstone ashlar, no mortar"; "rammed earth with lime plaster finish."
5. **Surroundings and context** — landscape, neighboring structures, people for scale (small, dressed for the period).
6. **View type** — pick one:
   - "Eye-level wide shot, photoreal architectural rendering"
   - "Three-quarter aerial view (~30° down), photoreal architectural rendering"
   - "Orthographic front elevation, measured drawing style, no perspective"
   - "Orthographic top-down plan, measured drawing style, with thin tone scale"
   - "Interior view from [position], photoreal"
7. **Lighting and atmosphere** — "low-angle dawn light from the east, long shadows, faint dust haze"; "high midday Mediterranean sun, hard shadows, deep cobalt sky"; "overcast even daylight, no shadows, museum-plate clarity."
8. **Style register** — "Photoreal architectural rendering, materially accurate, no fantasy embellishment, in the manner of a careful archaeological reconstruction (e.g. Balage Balogh or Jean-Claude Golvin)."
9. **Negative cues** — "no anachronisms, no modern figures, no power lines, no contemporary tourists, no fantasy spires, no exaggerated scale, no glowing magic, no lens flare, no tilt-shift miniature effect."

## Aspect ratio

- Wide exterior or urban view → `16:9`
- Three-quarter aerial or contextual view → `16:9` or `4:3`
- Front elevation → `4:3` or `16:9` depending on proportion
- Orthographic plan → `1:1` or `4:3`
- Interior portrait of a tall hall → `3:4`

## Worked example prompt

```
Ziggurat of Nanna at Ur, Third Dynasty of Ur, c. 2100 BCE, dawn.

Structure: three receding rectangular mudbrick terraces forming a stepped platform, monumental central staircase on the northeast face flanked by two convergent side stairs that meet at the first terrace, summit shrine of glazed cobalt-blue brick.

Material and construction: sun-dried mudbrick core faced with baked brick set in bitumen mortar, reed-mat layers visible between brick courses, weep-holes on the terraces, faint plaster traces.

Surroundings: flat alluvial plain, low temenos wall, a few small priestly figures in long fringed wool garments at the base of the staircase for scale, distant date palms.

View: three-quarter aerial view (~30° down) from the northeast.

Lighting: low-angle dawn light from the east, long warm shadows running west, faint dust haze in the air, deep cool sky overhead.

Style: photoreal architectural rendering, materially accurate, careful archaeological reconstruction in the manner of Balage Balogh / Jean-Claude Golvin.

Negative: no anachronisms, no modern figures, no power lines, no fantasy spires, no exaggerated scale, no glowing magic, no lens flare.
```

## Common follow-ups to offer

- A matched orthographic front elevation and top-down plan.
- A summit-level close-up showing the glazed shrine.
- The same building at twilight / by torchlight with priestly procession.
- A cutaway showing internal mudbrick coursing.
